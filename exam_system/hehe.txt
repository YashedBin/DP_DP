**The actual bug**, from the stack trace: `InvalidDataAccessApiUsageException: ... multiple keys`. Postgres's JDBC driver has a quirk — when you ask for generated keys via the generic `Statement.RETURN_GENERATED_KEYS` flag, it hands back the *entire inserted row*, not just the id. Spring's `GeneratedKeyHolder.getKey()` expects exactly one column and throws when it gets five.
